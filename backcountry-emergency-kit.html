What Belongs in a Backcountry Emergency Kit

Build a compact emergency kit around shelter, fire, water, signaling, first aid, repair, and communication.

Build Around Real Problems

A useful emergency kit should help with the problems most likely to turn a normal day into a bad night: injury, getting delayed after dark, losing your route, cold, dehydration, or equipment failure.

Core Items

  • Headlamp and spare power
  • Fire starter and dry tinder
  • Compact first-aid supplies
  • Emergency insulation or bivy
  • Water treatment
  • Navigation backup
  • Knife and repair tape

Communication

When you are outside reliable cell service, a satellite messenger can turn a serious emergency into a manageable rescue. Keep it accessible and know how to use the SOS function before the trip.

Rule: Carry enough to solve the most likely problems without turning the emergency kit into another full pack.
