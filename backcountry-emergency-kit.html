Survival

What Belongs in a Backcountry Emergency Kit

Build a compact emergency kit around shelter, fire, water, signaling, first aid, repair, and communication.

Build Around Problems, Not Gadgets

Think through the failures that could leave you cold, wet, injured, lost, or unable to communicate. Pack to solve those problems.

Core Categories

  • Emergency shelter
  • Reliable fire kit
  • Water treatment
  • Basic first aid
  • Light and signaling
  • Repair materials
  • Navigation and communication

Carry It Every Time

A perfect kit left in the truck is useless. Keep the essentials compact enough that they stay in the pack.

Backcountry Rule

Carry what solves a real problem. Leave the rest in the truck.
