<h2>Build Around Real Problems</h2><p>A useful emergency kit should help with the problems most likely to turn a normal day into a bad night: injury, getting delayed after dark, losing your route, cold, dehydration, or equipment failure.</p><h2>Core Items</h2><ul><li>Headlamp and spare power</li><li>Fire starter and dry tinder</li><li>Compact first-aid supplies</li><li>Emergency insulation or bivy</li><li>Water treatment</li><li>Navigation backup</li><li>Knife and repair tape</li></ul><h2>Communication</h2><p>When you are outside reliable cell service, a satellite messenger can turn a serious emergency into a manageable rescue. Keep it accessible and know how to use the SOS function before the trip.</p><div class="callout"><strong>Rule:</strong> Carry enough to solve the most likely problems without turning the emergency kit into another full pack.</div>
