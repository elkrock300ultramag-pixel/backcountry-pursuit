<h2>Protect Your Tinder</h2><p>In wet weather, the hardest part is getting the first small material to burn long enough to dry larger fuel. Carry dependable tinder in a waterproof bag.</p><h2>Find the Dry Wood Inside</h2><p>Dead standing branches, the underside of logs, and split wood can be much drier than exposed surfaces. Use a knife or small saw to reach the dry center when everything outside is soaked.</p><h2>Build Small Before Building Big</h2><p>Start with fine tinder and pencil-size fuel. Add larger pieces only after the small fire is producing enough heat to dry the next layer.</p><h2>Carry Redundancy</h2><p>A lighter, stormproof matches, and a ferro rod do not weigh much. Two independent ignition methods are worth carrying when fire may be part of your emergency plan.</p>
