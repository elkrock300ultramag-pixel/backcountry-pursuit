<h2>Think in Layers</h2><p>A cold-weather sleep system is more than a sleeping bag. Pad insulation, shelter, base layers, moisture control, and the bag all work together.</p><h2>Do Not Ignore the Ground</h2><p>The ground can pull heat away quickly. Use a sleeping pad with enough insulation for the temperatures you expect, and add a second pad when conditions call for it.</p><h2>Keep Insulation Dry</h2><p>Change out of wet hunting layers before bed when possible. Protect down insulation from rain and condensation and air it out when the sun gives you a chance.</p><h2>Plan for Colder Than Forecast</h2><p>Mountain temperatures can drop well below the forecast. Build a margin into your system and keep dry socks, a warm hat, and an emergency layer available.</p>
