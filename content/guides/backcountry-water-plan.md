<figure class="inline-photo"><img src="family-backcountry.webp" alt="Backcountry Water Planning"><figcaption>Backcountry Pursuit field photography.</figcaption></figure><p>Water planning starts before you leave the trailhead. Map dependable sources, questionable seasonal sources and backup options. Carry enough capacity to bridge the longest dry stretch.</p><h2>Do Not Depend on One Source</h2><p>Small springs and creeks can disappear late in the year. Identify multiple sources when possible.</p><h2>Treat Backcountry Water</h2><p>Use an appropriate filter, purifier or chemical treatment and follow its instructions. Clear-looking mountain water can still contain organisms that can make you sick.</p><h2>Protect Your Backup</h2><p>A lightweight treatment backup is worth carrying if your primary filter clogs, freezes or breaks.</p>
