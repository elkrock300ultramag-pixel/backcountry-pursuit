<h2>Keep the Kit Simple</h2><p>A basic trout kit should let you fish small streams, rivers, and mountain lakes without carrying a tackle store. Choose a few proven options and learn when to use them.</p><h2>Core Tackle</h2><ul><li>Small spinners and spoons</li><li>A few basic flies or nymphs</li><li>Light leaders or line</li><li>Forceps and nippers</li><li>Small assortment of hooks and split shot</li></ul><h2>Carry for the Water You Fish</h2><p>Small creeks need precise, compact presentations. Lakes and larger rivers may call for longer casts and slightly heavier tackle. Adjust the kit to the trip instead of carrying everything every time.</p>
