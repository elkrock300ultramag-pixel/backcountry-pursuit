<figure class="inline-photo"><img src="../assets/images/mule-deer.webp" alt="A Simple Mule Deer Glassing System"><figcaption>Backcountry Pursuit field photography.</figcaption></figure><p>Good glassing is a system. Pick a stable position, get comfortable and break the country into manageable sections instead of constantly sweeping the entire mountain.</p><h2>Start With High-Percentage Cover</h2><p>Check shade, benches, broken timber edges, brush pockets and terrain transitions first.</p><h2>Grid the Hill</h2><p>Use landmarks to divide the slope. Scan one section slowly, overlap the next pass and look for pieces of a deer—an ear, antler tine, leg or horizontal back line.</p><h2>Change Angles</h2><p>A move of a few hundred yards can reveal folds and pockets hidden from the first location.</p>
