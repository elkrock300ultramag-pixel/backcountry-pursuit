<h2>Fish Where Trout Can Rest and Feed</h2><p>Trout usually hold where current brings food close to slower water. Look for seams, pockets, undercut banks, drop-offs, and the soft water beside faster current.</p><h2>Start Close</h2><p>Many anglers cast over fish that are near their feet. Work the nearest good water first, then extend your casts farther across the stream.</p><h2>Use Depth and Cover</h2><p>Deeper slots, shade, submerged structure, and broken surface water give trout security. During bright conditions, these areas become even more important.</p><h2>Move Until You Find Active Fish</h2><p>Mountain streams reward mobility. If several good-looking pockets are empty, keep moving until you find the combination of temperature, flow, and insect activity that is holding fish.</p>
