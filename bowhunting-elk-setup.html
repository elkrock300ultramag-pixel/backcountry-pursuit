Bowhunting Elk: Closing the Last 100 Yards

Practical setup, wind, cover, and calling considerations for the final approach on a September bull elk.

Wind Is the First Decision

If the wind is wrong, the setup is wrong. Use terrain and thermals to approach from a direction that gives you room for the wind to drift without reaching the elk.

Get Ahead of the Elk

Whenever possible, set up where the elk wants to go instead of chasing from behind. Travel routes, benches, wallows, and bedding transitions create higher-percentage encounters.

Do Not Overcall

Calling can create the opportunity, but once the bull is committed, unnecessary sound can stop him or pull him off line.

Backcountry Rule

Carry what solves a real problem. Leave the rest in the truck.
