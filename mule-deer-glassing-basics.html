Mule Deer Glassing Basics

How to use terrain, light, shade, and patience to find western mule deer before committing to a long hike.

Glass Before You Walk

Use the first and last light to cover feeding areas, then shift attention to shaded pockets, broken timber, benches, and bedding cover.

Grid the Country

Do not let your eyes wander randomly. Break the hillside into small sections and work each one slowly. Look for ear tips, antler pieces, horizontal back lines, and color that does not match the terrain.

Let the Optics Save Your Legs

Every hour spent glassing from the right knob can prevent multiple miles of unnecessary hiking.

Backcountry Rule

Carry what solves a real problem. Leave the rest in the truck.
