Fishing

Build a Simple Trout Kit

A compact trout fishing kit that covers mountain streams, small rivers, family trips, and quick backcountry sessions without hauling a full tackle shop.

Keep the Core Small

Carry a few proven terminal options, spare leader or line, forceps, a small cutting tool, and a compact box that fits in one vest or pack pocket.

Cover Three Water Types

Have something for shallow riffles, something that can get deeper in pools, and something that can fish slowly around cover.

Do Not Forget the Non-Fishing Gear

Water, sun protection, a light layer, basic first aid, and a way to navigate matter more on remote water than carrying ten extra lure colors.

Backcountry Rule

Carry what solves a real problem. Leave the rest in the truck.
