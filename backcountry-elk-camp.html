ELK CAMP

Build a Backcountry Elk Camp That Works

A lightweight camp should help you recover every night without becoming a burden every morning.

Backcountry Pursuit Field Guide • Updated 2026

A good elk camp is not the camp with the most gear. It is the one that keeps you dry, lets you sleep, organizes essentials, and is easy enough to move when the elk move.

Shelter

Choose shelter based on season, exposure, bug pressure, and whether you value minimum weight or more weather protection.

Sleep System

Do not overlook the sleeping pad. Ground insulation is a major part of staying warm, especially during clear mountain nights.

Water

Know your water plan before committing to camp. Carrying several days of water can erase the weight savings from an ultralight setup.

Food

Prioritize calorie density, easy preparation, and foods you will actually eat when tired. Keep one fast meal available for late returns.

Camp Location

Avoid camping directly on the elk movement you intend to hunt. A slightly longer walk can preserve the area around feeding and bedding cover.
