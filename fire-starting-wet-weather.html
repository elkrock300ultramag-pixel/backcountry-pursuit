Survival

Reliable Fire Starting in Wet Weather

Redundant fire-starting methods, dry tinder strategy, and fuel preparation for wet or cold backcountry conditions.

Redundancy Is the Point

Carry more than one ignition method and keep at least one protected from water.

Bring Known-Dry Tinder

Do not assume the forest will provide easy tinder during rain or snow. A small amount of dependable tinder can save time and heat.

Prepare Fuel Before Lighting

Build a progression from fine dry material to progressively larger fuel so the fire has a chance to establish before being asked to dry wet wood.

Backcountry Rule

Carry what solves a real problem. Leave the rest in the truck.
