Welcome to From the Backcountry

A new field journal for short stories, current updates, scouting notes, fishing reports, camp life, and news from our time outside.

September 16, 2026 • From the Backcountry

Not every day outside needs to become a full field guide or a long hunting story. Sometimes the best things are the quick updates: what we are seeing in the mountains, what is happening on the water, a photo from camp, a lesson learned, or something worth sharing before the next trip.

From the Backcountry is where those updates will live. Expect short stories, scouting observations, fishing reports, gear notes, family adventures, seasonal news, and whatever else comes back with us from time outside.
