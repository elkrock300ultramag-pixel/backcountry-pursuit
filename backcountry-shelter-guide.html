How to Choose a Backcountry Shelter

A field-focused comparison of freestanding tents, trekking-pole shelters, floorless shelters, and when each makes sense.

Start With the Trip

Weather, bugs, elevation, terrain, and whether you are moving camp every day should decide shelter type before weight does.

Freestanding

Simple setup and dependable structure make freestanding tents easy to live with, especially in poor weather or rocky ground.

Trekking-Pole and Floorless

These options can save weight and offer excellent space, but they demand better site selection and more comfort with moisture, insects, and setup technique.

Backcountry Rule

Carry what solves a real problem. Leave the rest in the truck.
