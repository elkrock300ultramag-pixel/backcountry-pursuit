FAMILY FISHING

Getting Kids Into Fishing Without Making It Work

Keep the first trips simple, comfortable, and fun enough that they ask to go again.

Backcountry Pursuit Field Guide • 2026

Adults often measure fishing by the fish. Kids usually measure it by the entire day. Snacks, rocks, bugs, casting, wading, weather, and whether anyone is frustrated can matter more than the catch.

Start With Easy Water

Pick water with safe access and a realistic chance of action.

Keep the Gear Simple

One rod, a small tackle box, water, snacks, and weather layers are enough.

Let Them Explore

If they want to skip rocks for ten minutes, let them. The goal is building positive time outside.

Quit on a High Note

Leaving while they still want another cast is a win.
